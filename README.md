*BRIEF DESCRIPTION*: 

> The primary objective of this research is to develop a machine
> learning-based predictive model capable of the early identification of
> heart disease, or a high risk of developing it, in individuals based
> on a set of risk factors. Utilizing the provided dataset, the aim is

## Gym Enrollment Web App

*COURSE*: 

> **Processo e Sviluppo Software**

*BRIEF DESCRIPTION*: 

> The team designed and implemented a web application using the Spring MVC framework to manage a gym chain, aiming to effectively administer clients and Personal Trainers. The system governs client enrollment across the various facilities, providing members with the ability to obtain workout plans (a service included in the subscription fee) drafted by the different instructors. Personal Trainers can be assigned to one or multiple branches within the chain; similarly, clients can hold memberships at multiple gym locations. Every officially enrolled client or employed staff member at our facilities has the option to join the "Loyalty Card" initiative, which features a tiered point-accumulation system designed to offer redeemable benefits and rewards.
